# AI Study Assistant

A free study website by **Bilawal Ali Gul** (Founder) and **Dilbar Hussain** (Co-Founder), supervised by **Sir Majid Gul** (Knowledge Information Rising Stars, Wahi Pandhi, Dadu, Sindh, Pakistan).

## What it is

A free, mobile-friendly study site for Pakistani students:

- **48 subjects** with key notes, MCQs, flashcards and video lectures
- **Entry test preparation** — 40 tests × 200 MCQs (MDCAT, ECAT, CSS and more)
- **Government jobs preparation** — practice packs, GK library, interview guidance and roadmaps
- **CA (Chartered Accountancy, ICAP)** — full stage-wise study section
- **Board past papers** for 27 boards
- **6 finance books** as page-by-page readers — The Intelligent Investor, Think and Grow Rich, The Richest Man in Babylon, Rich Dad Poor Dad, Just Keep Buying, The Psychology of Money
- **Ask AI** — answers from the site's own study library, plus a certificate generator and progress tracking (saved on the visitor's own device only)

## How it is hosted

This repository is published with **GitHub Pages** (free static hosting). The site is a single self-contained `index.html` — all photos and assets are inlined, so there are no separate files to manage.

## Notes

- The study content is the creators' own work and summaries written in their own words. Book sections are chapter-wise summaries, not the books' original text.
- No user data is collected or stored on any server; progress, flashcards and notes are saved only on the visitor's own device.
- The site makes no claim of being a government website and its certificate is not a government certificate.
